Our Story & Heritage

Rooted in Earth, Elevated to the Moon

Named after the celestial rhythm of the moon (Luna), Lunara was conceived as an ode to stillness in an increasingly noisy world.

Located in the tranquil enclave of Weerawila in Hambantota, our resort is surrounded by sacred waters and ancient migration flyways. When building Lunara, not a single mature tree was felled. Our stilted pavilions gently wrap around indigenous teak, kumbuk, and palu trees, elevated up to 9 meters high to leave the forest floor undisturbed.

The golden stag upon our crest represents the gentle sambar deer that graze beneath our balconies at dusk, while the full moon symbolizes our commitment to quiet harmony, unhurried time, and restorative rest.
